# Privacy Policy

**Version 2026-09-01**

French Voyage Akademie is a free educational service with no commercial purpose. This policy explains
what data is processed, why, and how to exercise your rights.

## 1. Data controller

Raul Antonio Gamero Navarrete — French Voyage Akademie.
Contact: raul.a.gamero@gmail.com

## 2. Data we process

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | Sign-in identifier and account recovery | Performance of the service (Art. 6.1.b) |
| Display name | Personalise the interface | Performance of the service |
| Password (BCrypt hash) | Authentication | Performance of the service |
| Interface language | Show the service in your language | Performance of the service |
| Progress and exercise answers | Resume where you left off and measure what you have learned | Performance of the service |
| Last sign-in date | Account security | Legitimate interest |

**What we never ask for:** nationality, country of residence, immigration or administrative status,
date of birth, postal address, phone number, payment details. This omission is deliberate: the
platform serves people whose administrative situation may be precarious, and data that is never
collected can neither leak nor be requisitioned.

## 3. Audience measurement

Traffic is measured with **Matomo**, installed on our own server. IP addresses are anonymised and no
data is sent to any third party. No advertising cookies, no social network trackers, no external
analytics service are used.

## 4. Recipients

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data is hosted on a virtual private server
rented from OVHcloud, within the European Union.

## 5. Retention

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, the account,
your progress and your answer history are erased permanently and immediately.

## 6. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability
(GDPR, Articles 15 to 21).

- **Access and rectification**: from the "My profile" page.
- **Erasure**: the "Delete my account" button, immediate and irreversible.
- **Any other request**: raul.a.gamero@gmail.com.

You may also lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

## 7. Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored as BCrypt hashes and are never readable, not
even by the service administrator. Encrypted database backups are taken daily.

## 8. Minors

The service is open from the age of 16. Below that age, the consent of a parent or guardian is
required.

## 9. Changes

Any new version is published on this page together with its date. Substantial changes are announced
the next time you sign in.
